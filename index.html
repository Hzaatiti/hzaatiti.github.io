What I'm working on

With Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi

Epilepsy in the brain

A joint project between the MEG lab at NYU Abu Dhabi and the epilepsy team at Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi. Magnetoencephalography picks up the magnetic fields of brain activity from outside the head, which makes it a way to look closely at where epileptic activity starts.

  • Read the NYU Abu Dhabi story, October 2026

NYU Abu Dhabi, since 2024

NeuroWaves

The documentation, experiment code and processing pipelines behind the MEG and EEG labs. It covers how data is recorded, checked against the BIDS standard and analysed, so that a study can be rerun by someone who was not in the room.

With Stanford and Inria, 2018 to 2022

Geomstats

An open-source Python package for geometry and statistics on manifolds. I was its third contributor, working on graph-structured data and on learning methods for automata.
