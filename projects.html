Epilepsy in the brain

A collaboration between the MEG lab at NYU Abu Dhabi and the epilepsy team at Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi (CCAD).

Magnetoencephalography (MEG) measures the weak magnetic fields produced by electrical activity in the brain, millisecond by millisecond and with nothing touching the patient. In epilepsy that matters because the brief spikes that appear between seizures can be traced back to the patch of cortex producing them, which is the question a clinical team needs answered.

NeuroWaves

NeuroWaves is the documentation, experiment code and processing pipelines for everyone who uses the MEG and EEG labs at NYU Abu Dhabi. It sets out how brain imaging data is recorded, validated and analysed across the lab, so that results can be reproduced.

My part in it:

  • Build and maintain the MEG and EEG processing pipelines, from raw signals to analysis-ready data.
  • Write the lab documentation on Read the Docs, covering acquisition, preprocessing and analysis.
  • Keep datasets BIDS-compliant with automated validation, and run continuous tests on the pipelines.
  • Design experimental paradigms and keep the MEG, EEG and OPM systems calibrated and ready for research.

Geomstats

Geomstats is a Python package for computations, statistics and machine learning on manifolds. One half is geometry: manifolds, Lie groups, fibre bundles, shape spaces and Riemannian metrics. The other half is learning algorithms for data that lives on curved spaces. It runs on NumPy, Autograd or PyTorch.

I joined as the third contributor while at IRT SystemX, and worked on:

  • Representing data on manifolds, mainly graph-structured data.
  • Learning methods for models of complex systems, including simple, timed and hybrid automata.
  • The core geometry and learning APIs, and their tests across backends.
